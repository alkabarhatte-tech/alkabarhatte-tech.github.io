About

An engineering educator who likes to put a working lab behind every lecture.

Dr. Alka Surendra Barhatte is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University, Pune, where she has taught since 2003. She teaches programming, data structures, artificial intelligence and machine learning to undergraduate and postgraduate students.

She runs the department's software programming labs, built its automotive electronics lab, and has designed more than a dozen course syllabi. Her PhD, from Savitribai Phule Pune University in 2020, was on detecting and classifying cardiac events with a neuro-fuzzy approach. She is an approved PhD supervisor and has guided more than 40 student projects.

Research

Signals from the body, the road and the vehicle, read with machine learning.

Research areas

  • Biomedical signal processing

    ECG analysis, QRS detection, arrhythmia and ischemia classification using wavelets, curvelets, ICA, SVM, CNN and neuro-fuzzy (ANFIS) models, including smartphone-based ECG acquisition.

  • Deep learning for intelligent transportation

    Pothole detection, real-time traffic control and monitoring, and driver assistance systems.

  • Automotive electronics and electric vehicles

    Battery life under different driving styles, remotely drivable electric vehicles, and neural-network control of switched reluctance motor drives.

  • IoT, embedded systems and edge AI

    Low-power sensing and inference on microcontrollers such as ESP32 and STM32.

  • Machine learning for healthcare

    Postpartum depression detection, and a joint project with a dental college on predicting maxillary canine impaction.

Current projects

  • A lightweight, edge-deployable medical large language model for resource-constrained healthcare settings.
  • An offline retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system for edge AI applications.
  • Low-cost wearable AR translation glasses using an ESP32-S3 and cloud-based speech translation.
